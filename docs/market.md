## Alternatives shops use today

| Alternative | What's wrong with it |
|---|---|
| Wholesalers' Telegram channels | Prices are scattered across dozens of channels, go stale fast, and can't be compared or tracked over time |
| Calling suppliers | About 2 days a week of the owner's time (`product.md` §1, from the user persona; **needs verification** with 2–3 real shop owners) |
| Distributors' sales agents | Show only their own price list, so there's no comparison |
| Going to the bazaar (Chorsu, Alay, etc.) | A day lost, and the price depends on the stall and on bargaining |

No one combines prices from all sources, shows price history, or picks the cheapest mix of suppliers including delivery.
